   - *Why It Matters:* Chen Daofeng group papers (Lu 2018 PMC5925397 + Li 2025 PMC12254813 + Yu 2026 PMC12937656) show HCP/HCPM inhibits classical/lectin/alternative-pathway complement (CH50 79–318 µg/mL) at CP0 and TLR4-MD2/NF-κB/NLRP3 at CP1. This is the first dietary compound in the corpus with *direct* evidence at both the upstream priming and complement-initiation steps — orthogonal to the mushroom β-glucan (Dectin-1) and small-molecule (NLRP3 Cys279) axes. However, Cheng 2014 (PMC7112369) shows the 60 kDa HCP-2 fraction is **pro-inflammatory on naïve PBMCs** via TLR4; the anti-inflammatory phenotype emerges only in disease-context inflammation. This structure- and context-dependent directionality is a platform-level pattern that applies to all mushroom β-glucans and many TCM polysaccharides — the same caveat that forced the Phase 5 re-scan of *Phellinus* and *Sanghuangporus*. The dietary-CP0 strategy (comp-039) now has a clear "use the purified HCPM fraction, not generic extract" requirement.
