# The GSDMD pore self-delivery paradox (`gsdmd-pore-delivery-paradox.

The GSDMD pore self-delivery paradox (`gsdmd-pore-delivery-paradox.md`) is the most fascinating and underexplored mechanism in the corpus. The idea that an inflammatory cell's death-pore becomes a Trojan horse for its own medicine is deeply elegant. The specific connection to KPV peptide (`kpv-peptide.md`, Connection #3 above) is the most compelling application. KPV is small, hydrophilic, and hits the exact intracellular targets (NLRP3/NF-kB) that are active in a pyroptotic cell. The synergy is perfect on paper. The refuting evidence would be data from the proposed experiment (Proposed Experiments #1) showing that either the pores are too short-lived for meaningful influx or that KPV is degraded too quickly in the extracellular space. The cheapest experiment to discriminate is the proposed in vitro fluorescent-KPV uptake study. If that single experiment is positive, it opens up an entirely new class of targeted therapies for acute inflammatory conditions, where drugs are selectively delivered only to the cells that are actively dying and propagating inflammation. This feels like a genuinely novel insight with leverage far beyond just gout.

> **Pass 3 review — Confirmed, prioritize.** `[OVERLAP: NOVEL]` This is the most elegant mechanism in the corpus, and the synthesizer's articulation captures why. The GSDMD pore self-delivery paradox (`gsdmd-pore-delivery-paradox.md`) is genuinely underexplored — the 2025 bioRxiv preprint describes it for caspase inhibitors, but no gout program has applied it to anti-inflammatory payloads. The KPV connection (Connection #3) is the cleanest instantiation: KPV is small, hydrophilic, hits intracellular NLRP3/NF-κB, and its membrane-impermeance under baseline conditions means it would be *selectively* concentrated in pyroptotic cells during a flare. The refutation path is also crisp — the proposed fluorescent-KPV uptake experiment directly tests whether pore lifetime exceeds the diffusion time needed for meaningful intracellular accumulation. If that experiment returns positive, the therapeutic class it opens (pore-directed delivery of OE biologics) extends beyond gout to sepsis, ARDS, NASH, and CAPS — exactly the leverage the synthesizer claims. Elevate to `Confirmed, prioritize.` because the combination of mechanistic elegance, low-cost discriminability (~$2,000–5,000 to falsify), and cross-indication leverage makes this the highest-ROI thread to pull next.

Priority is now conditional on identifying that payload; Ac-FLTD-CMK is the lead candidate.
