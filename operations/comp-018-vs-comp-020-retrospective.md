# Method Retrospective on Brief Contamination

## Incident

A contrived plant example from Brian's scope clarification was copied into the COMP-018 research brief. The resulting report made the corresponding plant-associated compound its headline and echoed Brian's framing back to him. Brian challenged the narrative fit and requested an independently scrubbed re-run.

COMP-020 kept the research scope and method but removed compound names, predictions, and motivational examples. The two runs differed enough in candidate coverage and framing to show that the original brief had influenced the answer.

This record establishes a methodological failure and its correction. It does **not** preserve either COMP's candidate tiers, cross-paper potency comparisons, assay-format causality, mechanistic verdicts, or platform priorities. COMP-018 is [invalidated and non-runnable](../wiki/etc/experiments/comp-018-upstream-complement-modulator-sweep/). Current scientific evidence belongs on the [COMP-020 evidence page](../wiki/upstream-complement-verification-rerun-computational.md) and each candidate's evidence home.

## Control introduced

**Scope and method propagate; predictions and contrived examples do not.**

Keep in a delegated research brief:

- the weakness or mechanism being investigated;
- inclusion and exclusion criteria;
- source, language, evidence, and verification requirements;
- known facts needed to avoid duplicate work;
- required outputs and decision rules.

Remove from an independence-sensitive brief:

- illustrative compound, organism, or modality names that are not part of the real scope;
- predictions about what should win;
- motivational metaphors and rhetorical framing;
- user phrasing that invites narrative callbacks.

A useful check is to ask whether an independent researcher could perform the same investigation without the disputed phrase. If yes, remove it. If a report echoes the phrase back or produces an unusually neat narrative fit, run a context-isolated verification brief before promotion.

## Closure

The incident changed authoring practice, not scientific candidate authority. A scrubbed re-run can reveal prompt sensitivity, but convergence or divergence between two agent reports is not biological replication. Scientific claims still require their primary sources and, where load-bearing, empirical validation.

Git retains the fuller historical narrative. The live corpus keeps only the operational lesson.
