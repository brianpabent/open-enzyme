# Wet-Lab Collaboration Leads & Outreach Strategy

This document maps the global academic landscape to find research partners for executing **Experiment 1.9 (Ward 1995 dual-cassette feasibility test)** and **Experiment 1.25 (DAF SCR1-4 single-cassette expression)**. These two experiments represent the project's **#1 priority gate**, deciding whether the single-strain *A. oryzae* koji endgame thesis stands or if the project must fall back to a two-strain co-fermentation model.

---

## Technical Profile of the Experiment Partner

To execute §1.9 and §1.25, a collaborator's lab must possess:
1. **Aspergillus oryzae genetic engineering tools** (protoplast-mediated transformation, PEG/CaCl₂, or Agrobacterium-mediated transformation).
2. **Solid-state fermentation (SSF) capability** (growing mold on steamed rice/rice bran under controlled humidity and temperature).
3. **The NSlD-ΔP10 protease-deletion strain** (from the University of Tokyo) or the capacity to construct equivalent protease deletions.
4. **Standard analytical assays** (Western Blot, ELISA for heterologous titers, spectrophotometric uricase activity assays, and HPLC/LC-MS for kojic acid/ergothioneine baselines).

## Tier 3: Uricase Enzyme Specialists (Expression, Engineering & In Vivo Models)

## Strategic Outreach Framework

Since Brian does not have a formal biochemistry PhD background, **having a PhD-level collaborator (like Lauren or a recruited advisor) initiate contact** will increase response rates. The message must be direct, academically rigorous, and transparent about our open-source, non-profit approach.

### Key Asks
1. **Ask 1 (Minimum Viable - MTA):** Request the *A. oryzae* NSlD-ΔP10 strain under a standard academic Material Transfer Agreement.
2. **Ask 2 (Full Collaboration):** Propose a co-authored academic study or fee-for-service arrangement where their lab performs the transformations and fermentation assays, funded by our seed capital ($5K - $15K budget per batch).

### Technical Hooks for the Email
* **Reference Specific Precedents:** Cite Huynh et al. 2020 ( adalimumab production in NSlD-ΔP10) to establish context.
* **Address the Chaperone Burden (comp-010/comp-030):** State that our in silico folding model predicts the dual-cassette (lactoferrin + uricase) endoplasmic reticulum folding burden is ~1.06× that of their published adalimumab work.
* **Open Source & Open Science:** Provide links to our public research repository, showing that all construct designs, hypotheses (H01, H05), and protocols are fully documented.
