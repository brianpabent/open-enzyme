# Wet-Lab Collaboration Leads & Outreach Strategy

This document maps the global academic landscape for **Experiment 1.9 (Ward 1995 staged lactoferrin/UOX/dual-cassette feasibility)** and **Experiment 1.25 (DAF SCR1-4 single-cassette expression)**. After comp-044/045, §1.33 is the gout program's Gate 0; §1.9 remains the conditional #1 koji-endgame architecture gate. Partner outreach remains active because the §1.9A lactoferrin-only arm can run while §1.33 selects UOX topology, and the same chassis/infrastructure later supports §1.9B/C and §1.25.

---

## Technical Profile of the Experiment Partner

To execute §1.9 and §1.25, a collaborator's lab must possess:
1. **Aspergillus oryzae genetic engineering tools** (protoplast-mediated transformation, PEG/CaCl₂, or Agrobacterium-mediated transformation).
2. **Solid-state fermentation (SSF) capability** (growing mold on steamed rice/rice bran under controlled humidity and temperature).
3. **The NSlD-ΔP10 protease-deletion strain** (from the University of Tokyo) or the capacity to construct equivalent protease deletions.
4. **Standard analytical assays** (Western Blot, ELISA for heterologous titers, spectrophotometric uricase activity assays, and HPLC/LC-MS for kojic acid/ergothioneine baselines).

# Broader Hyperuricemia Field Map (expanded per request)

Tiers 1–3 above are **execution partners** — labs that could actually run our koji/uricase experiment. The **gut-microbiome axis (Tier 4) is the most thesis-adjacent** — those groups are doing, in different chassis, the same thing we are (engineering gut microbes to clear luminal urate).
