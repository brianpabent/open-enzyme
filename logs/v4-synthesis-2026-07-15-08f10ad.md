The single thread I would spend the next experiment slot on is the **gut-lumen uricase physiological regime × topology × oxygen × peroxide factorial (validation §1.33 + comp-044/045)**. Corpus evidence supporting the hunch: comp-044 invalidated comp-019’s flat-dose regime after restoring the 0.59 µM luminal urate and Km = 25 µM inputs that comp-019 stored but never used (wiki/gut-lumen-uricase-physiologic-regime-computational.md; also comp-019 flux-model-summary.md); comp-045 showed no topology can yet be eliminated and that the decisive comparison must measure urate, product, O2, H2O2, viability, and localization together under the same conditions (wiki/uricase-topology-oxygen-peroxide-design-computational.md). Evidence that would refute it: reproducible product formation at the human-baseline substrate prior with no extracellular H2O2 or viability penalty across all topologies. Cheapest discriminating experiment: the 19 non-duplicative factorial already designed in comp-045 (three independent biological runs, separate oxic/microoxic plates, matched inactive-UOX/chassis/PULSE-mixture controls, urate/product/H2O2/dissolved-O2/viability/localization readouts) — ~$5-8K and 6–8 weeks in a partner lab.
