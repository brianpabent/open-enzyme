## Implementation and constraint closure

I traced `inputs/model_parameters.json` → `analyze.py` → `outputs/results.json` / `outputs/summary.md`.

**Implementation trace**

- `capacity_ratio()` computes:

  `dose_mg × specific_activity_U_per_mg × pH_factor × 60 min/h × hours × urate/(Km+urate) × oxygen × access × survival`

  then converts µmol urate to mg with MW 168.11 g/mol and divides by `legacy_intestinal_urate_flux_mg_per_day = 233`.

- Unit handling is internally coherent:
  - U/mg = µmol/min/mg.
  - µmol × 168.11 g/mol / 1000 = mg.
  - The central diagnostic result is reproducible by inspection: 5 mg × 8.3 × 0.75 × 60 × 3 × 0.59/(25+0.59) × 168.11/1000 / 233 = ~0.0932.
- Grid size is correct: 5 urate × 3 Km × 4 hours × 3 oxygen × 3 access × 3 survival = 1,620 per dose.
- The committed output values appear consistent with the script logic. I did not execute the code.

**Stored-but-unused / duplication findings**

- `measured_or_regulatory_priors` is not used by executable code. Its central/low/high values are manually duplicated into `named_scenarios` and `exhaustive_grid`.
- `scenario_only_values_not_measured_human_baselines` is also not used directly; its values are manually duplicated into the executable scenario/grid sections.
- `model_purpose` and `inputs/query-strategy.json` are documentation-only.
- This is not automatically a bug, but it is a maintenance risk: changing the documented priors would not change the scenarios or outputs unless the duplicated values are also edited. The artifact should either derive scenarios from the prior blocks or explicitly label those blocks as documentation-only and add consistency assertions.

**Question/model fit**

The computation answers a narrower question than a physiological UOX efficacy model:

- It does test whether comp-019’s Vmax-style 24 h saturated-capacity shortcut survives when urate substrate occupancy and finite exposure time are applied.
- It does **not** determine the real in vivo urate-degradation rate, because it holds luminal urate concentration fixed without modeling local volume, replenishment, depletion, ABCG2 delivery, longitudinal gut movement, diffusion, reabsorption, or renal compensation.
- The key hidden substitution is still present but now disclosed: nominal enzyme capacity × Michaelis–Menten occupancy is used as a regime diagnostic, not as a physiological reaction flux.
- Oxygen is a required substrate/electron acceptor for uricase but is represented as a dimensionless linear scenario multiplier, not by measured O₂ concentration or oxygen Km/transport.
- The denominator is whole-day legacy intestinal flux, while the numerator is a local fixed-concentration active-window capacity. That comparison is useful as an audit of comp-019’s shortcut but is not a closed mass-balance.

**Constraint closure**

- **Reaction closure:** Uricase consumes urate + O₂ + H₂O and produces 5-hydroxyisourate/allantoin pathway products plus CO₂ and H₂O₂. Code tracks only urate-equivalent capacity. It does not compute O₂ consumption, allantoin/product formation, CO₂, or H₂O₂ burden.
- **Substrate regime:** Urate occupancy is explicitly modeled. At 0.59 µM vs Km 25 µM, occupancy is ~2.3%, which is the central correction.
- **Cofactor / cosubstrate regime:** O₂ is included only as a scenario multiplier; no physical O₂ mass balance or local oxygen kinetics.
- **Mass balance and time:** The active window is included, but substrate replenishment and depletion are not. Ratio = 1 is the only physically meaningful boundary, as the artifact correctly states.
- **Localization / access:** Access is a nonmechanistic multiplier. No topology, mucus diffusion, cell-associated vs secreted enzyme, or particle geometry is modeled.
- **Safety / coproducts:** H₂O₂ is not calculated. Local peroxide peaks, antioxidant urate loss, epithelial injury, catalase localization, and off-target redox burden are deferred to validation §1.33 / §1.36.
- **Sensitivity coverage:** The grid covers urate, Km, hours, oxygen, access, and survival, but not pH factor, specific activity, denominator uncertainty, local volume, replenishment rate, reabsorption, enzyme decay kinetics, topology covariance, or O₂ kinetics. The chosen grid is a scenario occupancy map, not a probability distribution.

## New connections or implications

- The central 50 mg no-extra-penalty ratio is close to one, so the next wet-lab gate should not only classify “works/does not work”; it should measure actual product formation with enough precision to resolve near-boundary regimes.
- Because O₂ is a uricase substrate, not merely an environmental nuisance, §1.33 should measure dissolved oxygen and product/allantoin or equivalent oxidative product together. Urate disappearance alone is not enough if adsorption, reabsorption, or assay interference can mimic consumption.
- H₂O₂ safety and urate antioxidant-loss are not secondary add-ons; they are directly coupled to every mole of successful UOX turnover. This strengthens the need for validation §1.36 after topology selection.
- The artifact’s contrast between jejunal low-substrate and distal high-substrate/anoxic cases reinforces that topology selection and gut segment cannot be separated. A topology that wins at high distal substrate may still fail if oxygen collapses, and a topology that wins proximally may be substrate-starved.
- The manually duplicated prior/scenario structure is a general pattern risk for future comp artifacts: “evidence prior” blocks can become decorative unless executable scenarios are generated from them or checked against them.
