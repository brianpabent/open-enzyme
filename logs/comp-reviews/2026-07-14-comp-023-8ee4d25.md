# Independent comp review — comp-023

## Bottom-line verdict

**Action required.** The qualitative conclusion “Jeennor-scale cns1+cns2 is unlikely to be a prohibitive *stoichiometric FBA* burden” is plausible, but the artifact-summary contract is not clean. The stress-test breakpoint, carbon-fraction claim, reproduction path, and several wiki propagations need correction. The model is a simplified steady-state stoichiometric proxy, not a demonstrated therapeutic-dose or multi-cassette-home-fermentation feasibility result.

## Implementation and constraint closure

I traced the main load-bearing path:

- `README.md` states the question, thresholds, scenario set, and intended method.
- `analyze.py` loads iWV1314, modifies media, adds manual kojic acid / ergothioneine / cordycepin / carnosine / panD reactions, adds fixed protein-burden drains, runs FBA, then writes `outputs/results.json` and `outputs/summary.md`.
- `outputs/results.json` matches the main summary table for growth, max kojic, max EGT, and cordycepin flux.
- Current top-level wiki pages partly reconcile the target as deprioritized, but several surfaces still carry stale “green-light §1.9 cordycepin arm” framing or unsupported quantitative numbers.

Key closure findings:

1. **Question/model fit is only partial.**  
   The computation asks whether fixed production demands create stoichiometric FBA burden at a selected titer. It does **not** resolve:
   - whether home/solid-state koji can reach the Jeennor fed-batch titer;
   - whether the titer corresponds to a meaningful dose;
   - whether ADA competition, cns expression levels, promoter interference, induction timing, or pentostatin protection allow cordycepin accumulation;
   - whether dual lactoferrin/uricase secretion stress affects cns expression dynamically.

2. **Reference-titer verdict is probably directionally robust, but the stress breakpoint is not reliable.**  
   At the empirical flux, `COR_SYN` is fixed at `0.018728 mmol/gDW/h`, which is small relative to 10 mmol/gDW/h glucose uptake. However, the reported “breakpoint around 1,000× empirical titer” is not physically trustworthy: at 1,000×, cordycepin export is `18.728 mmol/gDW/h`, i.e. ~187 C-mmol/gDW/h product carbon, far exceeding the 60 C-mmol/gDW/h from glucose uptake. A carbon-export demand larger than glucose-carbon input should not be treated as a meaningful physiological breakpoint. This points to model-cycle / manually added-reaction / mass-balance limitations in the stress tests.

3. **Carbon-fraction summary is arithmetically inconsistent.**  
   The corpus claims cordycepin demand consumes “~0.02% of cellular carbon flux.” Using the artifact’s own constants:
   - cordycepin flux = `0.018728 mmol/gDW/h`
   - cordycepin has 10 carbons → `0.1873 C-mmol/gDW/h`
   - glucose uptake = `10 mmol/gDW/h × 6 C = 60 C-mmol/gDW/h`
   - carbon fraction ≈ `0.1873 / 60 = 0.312%`

   Even using product mmol vs glucose mmol gives ~0.187%, not 0.02%. The qualitative “<1%” statement is fine; the specific `0.02%` number is not.

4. **Manual reactions and exchange demands are not fully mass/constraint audited.**  
   The cordycepin net reaction is simplified as:

   `adenosine + ATP + NADPH → cordycepin + ADP + Pi + NADP+ + H+`

   This is plausible as a pathway-level placeholder but is not shown to be element/charge balanced in the artifact. The stress-test results strongly suggest finite mass-balance should not be inferred from the LP outputs alone. Kojic and EGT reactions are also manual one-step approximations; the artifact owns this, but downstream summaries should not treat their capacities as native-yield predictions.

5. **Native metabolite “yield headroom” is individual, not simultaneous.**  
   `probe_native_metabolite_capacity()` separately maximizes `EX_KOJIC` and `EX_EGT` with biomass locked at ≥99% of scenario optimum. The decision table therefore shows separate capacities, not simultaneous kojic+EGT co-production. Wording like “kojic + EGT headroom” should be clarified as “each in separate re-optimization.”

6. **Protein-burden modeling contains hidden substitutions.**
   - Lactoferrin and uricase are modeled as fixed **glutamate + ATP** drains, not actual amino-acid-composition drains, despite the top docstring saying lactoferrin uses an amino-acid-composition-weighted pool.
   - Lactoferrin threshold `500 mg/L` is implemented as `500 mg/L/day` productivity over a 5 gDW/L culture. That is a time-base substitution: validation thresholds often refer to endpoint concentration, not daily productivity. This likely overestimates burden if the endpoint accumulates over multiple days, but it must be stated accurately.
   - PDI/chaperone residence time and ER saturation are not in the FBA; the artifact notes this, but some surfaces still overinterpret “GREEN” as an engineering green light.

7. **Stored / claimed outputs with no committed derivation.**
   The archived wiki claims that cordycepin taps adenosine via SAH hydrolase `r857` and substitutes for an ATP-wasting adenosine kinase step. `analyze.py` does not write the relevant flux split, `r857`, ADA fluxes, or adenosine kinase fluxes into `results.json`. That mechanistic explanation may be true in an inspected solution, but it is not reproducible from the committed outputs without rerunning and extracting flux distributions.

8. **Sensitivity coverage is convenient but incomplete.**  
   The demand sweep covers cordycepin flux by large multipliers, but does not explicitly vary:
   - biomass density used to convert titer to mmol/gDW/h;
   - glucose uptake bound;
   - oxygen limitation;
   - NADH vs NADPH cofactor choice;
   - ADA flux competition;
   - media closure choices;
   - simultaneous kojic+EGT production;
   - element-balanced alternatives for the cordycepin reaction.

## New connections or implications

1. **The comp-023 artifact accidentally demonstrates why “metabolically feasible” must stay separate from “therapeutically useful.”**  
   Current strategy already made this correction by routing cordycepin to the medicinal-mushroom cultivation track. The FBA result can remain a cytosolic-burden methodology precedent, but it should not be used as a wet-lab prioritization green light for koji-cordycepin.

2. **Stress-test outputs expose a general FBA guardrail for future cytosolic-cassette comps.**  
   Any future “breakpoint” claim should include an elemental carbon check: product carbon export cannot exceed substrate carbon input unless the model explicitly includes additional carbon sources. This simple guardrail would have caught the 1,000× breakpoint issue.

3. **Carnosine / other cytosolic third-cassette candidates should inherit the method, not the verdict.**  
   Comp-023 supports the idea that cytosolic cassettes can be evaluated orthogonally to ER/PDI burden, but future candidates need their own titer-to-dose conversion, finite substrate, and mass-balance audit.
