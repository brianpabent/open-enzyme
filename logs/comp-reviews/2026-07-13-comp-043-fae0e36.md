# Independent comp review — comp-043

## Bottom-line verdict

Action required — the artifact is largely reproducible by inspection and its **qualitative direction** is plausible, but the quantitative folding verdict rests on an inferred, unmeasured DsbA/DsbC capacity model; one interpretive-page number is stale/inconsistent with code/output; several primary-source verifications are claimed but not directly available in the artifact; and corpus surfaces should be tightened so EcN-DAF is framed only as a provisional secondary route, not as an LBP rescue or superiority claim.

## Implementation and constraint closure

I traced the core path:

- `inputs/disulfide_topology.json` → `connectivity_metrics()` → loop lengths, crossings, effective folding demand.
- Effective demand → `folding_axis()` using fixed capacity anchors 5/8/12 and the `folding_nonviability()` linear mapping.
- FASTA + AlphaFold pLDDT + `colonic_ecn_protease_panel.json` → shared `experiments/lib/protease_stability.py` → `protease_axis()`.
- Payload key → hard-coded categorical `glyco_axis()`.
- `fold`, `prot`, `glyco` → `composite_verdict()` → `outputs/results.json` and `outputs/summary.md`.

Key implementation findings:

- The committed `outputs/results.json` and `outputs/summary.md` are internally consistent with the provided `analyze.py` logic.
- The shared protease library does use dynamically named `P1_preferred`, `P1_prime_preferred`, `ph_activity_at_shio_koji`, and `salt_inhibition` fields, so many heuristic “unused JSON” leaves are false positives.
- Stored-but-unused / documentation-only fields remain important:
  - `active_pH_range`, `optimal_pH`, `pH_range`, `temperature_C`, and `duration_days` are not mechanistically integrated.
  - Protease exposure is a **site-risk scan**, not a residence-time cleavage model over 1–7 days.
  - Protease concentrations, enzyme turnover, substrate concentration, secretion rate, and replenishment are not represented.
- The protease model uses pLDDT as an accessibility proxy. The shared library itself warns this can misclassify confident exposed helices as “buried”; comp-043 discloses this limitation, but the RED/LOW protease numbers should remain secondary, not quantitative survival probabilities.
- The folding model is the load-bearing substitution: it does not model DsbA/DsbB/DsbC reaction kinetics, DsbB reoxidation, quinone/electron-acceptor availability, Sec translocation timing, periplasmic residence time, DegP competition, inclusion/aggregation, expression titer, or native-fold attainment. It scores an architecture-weighted disulfide **burden proxy** against an inferred capacity band.
- Reaction/constraint closure is incomplete by design:
  - Folding substrates/cofactors: reduced cysteines, DsbA oxidation, DsbC isomerization, DsbB/quinone reoxidation, periplasmic redox capacity are not explicitly mass-balanced.
  - Transport/localization: Sec/periplasm/outer-membrane export topology is not modeled; the optimistic SHuffle anchor is cytoplasmic and compartment-mismatched, correctly flagged but still included in the DAF band.
  - Residence/exposure time: colonic 1–7 day residence is stored in JSON but not used.
  - Safety/off-targets: envelope stress, DegP overload, misfolded human-protein fragments, OmpT/DegP local cleavage during secretion, and local redox burden are named limitations but not modeled.
- Sensitivity is narrow: the capacity anchors are varied, but the dominant arbitrary parameters — loop weights, crossing surcharge, decision thresholds, exclusion of SHuffle as a secreted-format anchor, and protease-time assumptions — are not fully stress-tested.

The model therefore supports a **bounded qualitative prior**: C1-INH is lower burden than DAF; DAF is much lower burden than lactoferrin; lactoferrin is implausible in EcN relative to koji. It does not establish a measured capacity crossover or a physiologic folding rate.

## New connections or implications

- comp-043 cleanly separates two failure modes often conflated in the corpus: **glycosylation absence** versus **oxidative-folding capacity**. For lactoferrin in EcN, the artifact supports “folding-limited first; missing glycans compound stability/protease risk” rather than “glycosylation kills function.”
- The EcN-side DsbA/DsbC capacity gap is structurally analogous to the koji-side α-coefficient gap in `chaperone-orthogonal-stacking.md`: both are unmeasured folding-capacity abstractions currently used to route chassis decisions.
- The strongest practical implication is not a new therapy claim; it is a wet-lab prioritization constraint: do not spend on EcN lactoferrin; keep DAF primarily on koji; if EcN DAF is pursued, the first measurement should be correct disulfide pairing + functional decay acceleration under DsbC-coexpression, not secretion alone.
- The artifact also reinforces that “GEM” is the wrong modeling class for folding-machinery competition; however, a future proteome/secretory-capacity or redox-capacity model could be useful if it explicitly includes DsbA/DsbB/DsbC turnover and periplasmic export, not metabolic flux alone.
