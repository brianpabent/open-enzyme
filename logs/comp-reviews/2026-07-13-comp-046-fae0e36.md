# Independent comp review — comp-046

## Bottom-line verdict

Action required — the artifact is directionally useful as a bounded algebraic design-space model, but the “endogenous 100-unit conserved ledger” and “machine-readable full-factorial results” contract is not fully implemented, and several corpus surfaces still contain stale or too-strong comp-031/PDB additivity wording.

The quantitative verdict is **not clinically interpretable** and does not estimate serum urate. Its valid contribution is narrower: it shows that (1) GR-5-like upstream nucleoside processing is beneficial only if coupled to retention or reduced absorption, and (2) UOX→PDB staging wins only under specified abstract capture/transfer/overlap assumptions.

## Implementation and constraint closure

I traced the provided `model_parameters.json` into `analyze.py`, `outputs/results.json`, `outputs/summary.md`, and the relevant wiki pages included in the bundle, then read additional affected pages: `purine-load-koji-vs-yeast.md`, `gut-lumen-sink.md`, `hypotheses/H08-gut-lumen-sink-platform-thesis.md`, `chassis-pending-interventions.md`, `uricase.md`, and the relevant `validation-experiments.md` sections. Repository grep was attempted but failed because `rg` is unavailable in the environment.

**Implementation closure:**

- The dietary precursor ledger is actually conserved:
  - 100 input units are partitioned into unintercepted absorbed/unabsorbed nucleoside, microbial salvage/retention, liberated absorbed base, and liberated unabsorbed base.
  - The central ledger arithmetic matches code and outputs: 36.25 + 13.75 + 22.5 + 18.940625 + 8.559375 = 100.
- The endogenous “ledger” is not implemented as a 100-unit conserved product/residual ledger:
  - `endogenous_luminal_urate_units` is stored in `model_parameters.json` but never used.
  - The code computes only capture fractions for well-mixed vs staged architectures.
  - It does not produce an explicit fate table of UOX product, PDB product, residual urate, inaccessible urate, or unaccounted material summing to 100.
- `architecture_equations` in JSON are documentation-only; the formulas are reimplemented separately in Python. They agree by inspection, but there is no parser/check tying the stored equations to the executable equations.
- `query-strategy.json` and non-executable JSON leaves are documentation-only; no issue if documented as such.
- `README.md` says `outputs/results.json` is “machine-readable full-factorial results,” but the committed `results.json` contains summaries only, not 6,561 per-cell rows. The full grid is reproducible by rerunning `analyze.py`, but not inspectable from committed outputs.
- The run path is plausible and deterministic: standard library only, fixed JSON inputs, no randomness. I did not execute code.

**Question/model fit:**

- The model does not resolve whether the biological system is genuinely complementary in vivo. It resolves a simplified algebraic question: under which assumed capture, overlap, transfer, salvage, and absorption fractions does one abstract architecture beat another?
- Hidden substitution: nominal “capture fraction” stands in for physiologic reaction rate. No UOX Km, PDB kinetics, substrate concentration, oxygen, electron acceptor availability, residence time, transport capacity, viability, or local replenishment is modeled.
- The upstream GR-5 component is appropriately framed as whole-cell cleavage plus salvage/retention, not DeoD alone. This is a strength.
- The dietary and endogenous pools are correctly not summed into ΔSUA. This is also a strength.

**Constraint closure:**

- **Dietary substrate/product closure:** adenosine/inosine/guanosine-like nucleosides are abstracted into one normalized “dietary purine” pool; free bases are abstracted into one “liberated base” pool. The code does not distinguish adenine, guanine, hypoxanthine, xanthine, urate, nucleotides, or nucleosides.
- **GR-5 operating regime:** no measured Km, transport rate, biomass capacity, microbial turnover, re-release, or intestinal residence time is modeled. `whole_cell_microbial_salvage_fraction` is a scenario assumption.
- **Absorption:** `free_base_absorption_relative_to_nucleoside` is multiplied by nucleoside absorption and capped at 1.0. This is computationally safe but not physiologically sourced in the artifact.
- **UOX closure:** UOX substrate is urate; O₂ and H₂O₂ are not modeled. Allantoin/5-HIU/further oxidative products are not tracked.
- **PDB closure:** anaerobic reductive pathway cofactors/electron handling, selenium/molybdenum dependence, intermediates, pyruvate/SCFA products, and viability are not modeled.
- **Localization/transport:** spatial staging is reduced to `staged_residual_transfer_efficiency`; same-pool competition is reduced to `well_mixed_shared_pool_overlap`. No diffusion, radial gut geometry, transit window, or longitudinal compartment volume is implemented.
- **Safety/off-targets:** H₂O₂, local urate antioxidant loss, PDB products, D-lactate, ammonia, redox burden, microbial persistence, and epithelial barrier injury are outside the computation.
- **Sensitivity ranges:** the ranges cover the convenient algebraic knobs and do identify dominant algebraic drivers, but they do not cover dominant physiological uncertainties such as Km/substrate occupancy, oxygen, electron acceptors, residence time, product toxicity, biomass retention duration, or transport capacity.

## New connections or implications

- The comp-046 dietary ledger reinforces the corrected `purine-load-koji-vs-yeast.md` stance: product total purine measurements alone are insufficient. The decisive measurement is species-resolved isotope fate through nucleosides, bases, biomass retention, urate appearance, and transepithelial transfer.
- The artifact makes §1.34 more than a “nice-to-have” experiment: it is required to distinguish nucleoside disappearance from actual purine removal.
- The endogenous architecture boundary connects directly to §1.33 and §1.37: UOX topology, oxygen/peroxide viability, and CBT2.0/PDB carbon fate cannot be interpreted independently if the same urate pool or residual transfer is not measured.
- The negative/near-zero median architecture difference means any corpus phrase implying automatic PDB+UOX additivity should be treated as stale unless backed by residual-transfer data.
