# Historical query-framing retrospective — method receipt

**Date:** 2026-05-19
**Status:** non-authoritative, incomplete retrospective
**Scientific findings:** none retained here

## Question

Did mechanism-name and ChEMBL-seeded searches miss candidate records that could
be retrieved through species names, exact material names, traditional formula
names, or traditional-pathology terms?

## Sources and methods attempted

- prior COMP and literature-workflow artifacts were sampled;
- PubMed/PubMed Central and general web search were used for counter-queries;
- CNKI/WanFang, J-STAGE/CiNii/J-GLOBAL, KISS/RISS, eLIBRARY.RU, and SciELO
  were considered where relevant;
- species, formula, traditional-pathology, and modern mechanism terms were
  combined for selected topics.

The pass did not retain a complete query ledger, immutable response sets,
deduplicated corpus, inclusion decisions, or an independently reproduced
coverage denominator. It therefore cannot establish recall, recovery rate,
field size, completed coverage, or that a topic needs no further search.

## Surviving methodological result

Alternative query framing retrieved additional candidate-specific records that
the earlier mechanism/database framing had missed. This invalidates any
empty-class conclusion produced solely by the earlier query method. It does not
show that the second pass was complete or biologically correct.

Use the four-frame search discipline in
[`wiki/etc/manual-literature-mining.md`](../wiki/etc/manual-literature-mining.md):
mechanism/target, species/material, traditional formula, and traditional
pathology. Record exact queries, sources attempted, counts, failures,
translation checks, and verification status in a compact JSON method receipt.

Candidate claims and conjectures belong on their owning wiki pages after
primary-source verification. Git retains the retired retrospective narrative.
