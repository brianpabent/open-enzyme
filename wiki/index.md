# Open Enzyme Research Library

**Use red-teaming techniques to identify exploitable weaknesses in gout, and use creative engineering to exploit them.**

Open Enzyme treats gout as an attack surface, not as a predetermined product brief. It maps causal and operational weaknesses, creates falsifiable intervention tracks, and promotes or kills them on evidence. [Mission and operating principles](./etc/open-enzyme-vision.md) is the canonical project definition.
