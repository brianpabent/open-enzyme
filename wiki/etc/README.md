# `wiki/etc/`

This directory holds material needed to reproduce or govern the research but not treated as independent scientific claims during every full synthesis:

- tool and source catalogs;
- authoring, literature, and validation methods;
- team and project-reference pages;
- static diagrams;
- reproducible `comp-NNN` code, inputs, outputs, provenance, and current reviews.

Active mechanistic findings, track state, hypothesis cards, and decisions belong in `wiki/` or `wiki/hypotheses/`. The concise mission page, [`open-enzyme-vision.md`](./open-enzyme-vision.md), is explicitly included in the scientific synthesis corpus.

## Computational artifacts

Each COMP has a top-level interpretive wiki page in the scientific corpus. Exact current outputs remain here. A synthesis candidate that relies on a COMP must reopen those outputs and verify its current push-review eligibility before promotion; the interpretive page alone is insufficient.

## Placement test

Put a page here when it explains *how to do or reproduce the work*. Put it in the scientific corpus when it says *what the current evidence means for a gout weakness or intervention track*.

Use `./etc/<page>.md` from `wiki/` and `../<page>.md` from this directory. Git is the history; do not add origin narratives or revision sections.
