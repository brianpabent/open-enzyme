# Independent disposition review — COMP-006

## Bottom line

The current verdict-producing experiment is not boundedly repairable. The shared library computes each site as accessibility weight × one per-protease effective-activity constant. Whenever a scope contains an “exposed” site, its maximum is therefore the same protease constant. The retained output confirms identical full, mature, ectodomain, and stalk maxima for every protease. The claimed scope comparison and stalk attribution do not follow from the model.

Replacing pLDDT with a defensible accessibility method would not repair that degeneracy. Answering a useful computational question would require a new scoring model, acceptance tests, outputs, and interpretation under a new COMP lifecycle. An exact-material matched wet-lab comparison remains the decisive route for the stalk-removal question; current §1.25 tests SCR1–4 without a full-ectodomain arm and therefore cannot attribute an outcome to stalk removal.

## Fatal result defects

- pLDDT confidence is treated as solvent accessibility; all exposure, burial, cleavage-risk, degradation, resistance, and survival interpretations are invalid.
- The maximum-risk construction is scope-degenerate and does not distinguish the full sequence, mature protein, ectodomain, or stalk in the retained result.
- HIGH/MODERATE/LOW thresholds are uncalibrated dimensionless labels and do not estimate degradation or retained activity.
- Several process-activity factors are analyst assumptions rather than measurements in the stated process, so the categorical verdict cannot carry the claimed meaning.

## Valid surviving material

- **Sourced inputs, pending primary-line verification where noted:** the committed P08174 sequence, AlphaFold confidence vector, and cited protein boundaries. These are inputs, not evidence of protease survival.
- **Deterministic inventory only:** recognition-pattern counts, region tallies, and pLDDT distribution statistics under the committed rules. They have no validated biological interpretation.
- **Research conjecture:** compare the full ectodomain with SCR1–4 in a matched assay to test whether removing the stalk changes recovery, folding, retained function, or process stability. Direct evidence for that leap remains absent.
