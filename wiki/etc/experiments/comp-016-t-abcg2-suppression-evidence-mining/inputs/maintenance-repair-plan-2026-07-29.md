# COMP-016 maintenance repair plan

## Defects in the prior artifact

- It treated a bounded, largely summary-tier search as evidence of broad
  literature absence.
- It hard-coded the direct-support count instead of deriving it from inputs.
- It collapsed genotype/sex, estradiol exposure, testosterone exposure,
  systemic urate, and non-intestinal cancer-cell evidence into one direction
  score.
- It attributed the 2023 Caco-2 paper to Klyushova instead of Slepnev.
- It retained unsupported Hoque 53%/88% intestinal reductions instead of the
  primary-verified 78% jejunal versus 44% renal Western comparison.
- It inferred a healthy male/female intestinal baseline and a platform ceiling
  that the input set did not test.
- It used invalidated COMP-014 as support for a fungal-ABCG2 frequency claim
  and a Ganoderma/2,4-DAE literature lead.
- It retained a vague cohort placeholder as if it were a citable paper.
- Its reproduction path was wrong.

## Repair

1. Preserve all 17 original inventory positions, exact search queries, and
   access failures.
2. Replace unsupported narrative fields with explicit source-verification
   tiers and target-test classifications.
3. Bind corrected Hoque, Liu, Slepnev, and MacLean findings to the primary or
   official-abstract verification record already established by COMP-017.
4. Keep the vague S15 row as a non-citable unresolved trace.
5. Derive all counts and the bounded result from the fixed JSON.
6. Emit no universal absence, healthy-human null, estradiol-baseline, male
   ceiling, clomiphene, or treatment claim.
7. Remove every COMP-014 dependency.
8. Require a same-context target outcome, a citable record, and a primary or
   official-abstract verification tier before any row can produce a
   demonstrated result.
9. Derive both positive and negative narratives from the computed result and
   direct-test rows so contrary evidence can win cleanly.

## Authorized output

The renderer may report only what the fixed classification decides, the four
source-corrected findings, the complete record-level boundary table, and the
experiment’s forbidden inferences. It may reject the old direct-suppression
premise only at the scope of the fixed inventory.

Any source reclassification, added literature, new retrieval, or new
biological model requires a new exact pre-run review.
