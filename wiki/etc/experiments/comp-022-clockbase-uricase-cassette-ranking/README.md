# comp-022 — *A. oryzae* Uricase Cassette Ranking

**Status:** Invalidated for candidate selection, component preference,
construct design, gene-synthesis routing, expression, processing, folding,
secretion, localization, activity, production, safety, and efficacy inference.

## What is invalidated

No numerical score, rank, count, N-of-four or N-of-five tier, shortlist,
top-cluster, winner, concordance claim, or composite verdict survives for
predictive or decision use.

The retired votes combined CAI, an mRNA-structure heuristic or ViennaRNA MFE,
an uncalibrated chaperone-load model, promoter × signal-peptide priors, and a
rescaled ESM2 log-probability proxy. These axes were not calibrated to one
named biological outcome. The chaperone-load coefficients, equal weighting,
quintile thresholds, and correlated-axis voting therefore cannot establish
expression, processing, native fold, secretion, active product, or function.

COMP-022 supplies no preference for PamyB, any signal peptide, a
5′-softened codon strategy, direct secretion, GlaA-KEX2 processing,
propeptide handling, terminal tags, PTS1 masking, N191Q, or any other
cassette component. Its gene-synthesis refinements are retired.

## Historical-output corrections

Within the retired v2 output, all four v1 top-cluster rows entered the
N-of-five ≥4 artifact shortlist, but only one of those four entered the strict
N-of-five =5 tier. The strict tier contained both PTS1-blocked and unblocked
routes. It therefore did not reproduce the v1 cluster or confirm PTS1
masking. Neither tier has predictive or decision authority.

The file historically named `esmfold_pLDDT.csv` contains single-pass ESM2
log probabilities rescaled to 50–90. It is not ESMFold pLDDT and does not
measure fold quality.

The retired inputs repeatedly call Q00511 residues 191–193 `NSS`. The bound
sequence and the current reviewed [UniProt Q00511](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q00511/entry)
record contain `NFS`. N191Q would disrupt that N-X-S sequon, but COMP-022 did
not establish that the mutation should be used.

## What survives

The historical program enumerated a declared 43,200-row Cartesian product.
That is an inventory fact, not evidence that the part universe was complete,
correctly parameterized, or suitable for construction.

Direct secretion and GlaA-KEX2 processing remain distinct, unranked
engineering configurations. Promoter, signal-peptide, codon, terminal
handling, propeptide, and glycosylation choices may remain prespecified
experimental factors, but COMP-022 selects none of them.

The [current evidence page](../../../uricase-cassette-ranking-computational.md)
routes the surviving construct question to matched configuration experiments.
Those experiments must measure transcript, processing fidelity, localization,
native or oligomeric state, fraction-specific intact active uricase, oxygen
and peroxide behavior, and host viability before advancing an exact construct.

Retirement does not reject uricase, *A. oryzae*, direct secretion, carrier
processing, or the wider gout exploit portfolio. It removes only the authority
of this ranking model.
