# comp-031 — Dual-chassis EcN PDB + uricase additivity

**Status:** Invalidated and superseded for biological or decision use.

## What survives

- One-strain, separate-strain, and temporally staged UOX/PDB designs remain unranked experimental options. COMP-031 supplies no evidence for choosing among them.
- Possible UOX/PDB complementarity remains a research question on the [current evidence page](../../../dual-chassis-ecn-pdb-uricase-computational.md), not a surviving COMP-031 result.

## What is invalidated

The former YELLOW verdict, every ΔSUA and additivity estimate, the substrate-competition result, the CBT2.0-derived-butyrate assumption, the Q141K-rescue effect, and the separate-strain recommendation are retracted.

The model inherited COMP-019's unconditional flat-dose/saturated-capacity UOX classification, transferred a *C. sporogenes* butyrate assumption into CBT2.0/EcN, misattributed direct butyrate rescue to Basseville 2012, used unmatched background butyrate, and treated spatially distinct oxidative and anaerobic processes as well-mixed competitors. COMP-044 invalidates only COMP-031's reliance on that inherited classification: it shows that the classification is not robust to the tested substrate-occupancy and finite-window diagnostics. It does not invalidate every possible UOX operating regime or the separate conjecture that UOX and PDB could be complementary.
